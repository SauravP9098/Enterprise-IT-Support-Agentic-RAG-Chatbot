# KB01 - BitLocker Issue

## Purpose
This KB explains how to retrieve a BitLocker recovery key using the One Identity portal. 

## Steps

### Step 1: Log in to One Identity
- Open the One Identity portal.
- Log in using your GUID and CyberArk-generated password. 

### Step 2: Switch Role and Search Device
- Change your account role to **Territory Support**.
- Search for the user's device using the laptop hostname. 

### Step 3: Retrieve the BitLocker Key
- All devices associated with the searched hostname will be displayed.
- Select the appropriate system/device.
- View the BitLocker recovery key from the device details. 

## Expected Outcome
The BitLocker recovery key can be obtained from the selected device record in One Identity.
