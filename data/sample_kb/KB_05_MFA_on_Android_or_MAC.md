# KB05 - Modern Authentication Journey on Android / MacBook

## Purpose
This KB provides steps to configure Microsoft Authenticator for Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) on Android and macOS devices, as well as instructions for migrating MFA to a replacement mobile device.

## Initial MFA Setup

### Step 1: Access MFA Setup
- Open a browser on your laptop or PC.
- Navigate to: https://aka.ms/mfasetup
- Sign in using your PwC credentials.
- Click **Next**.

### Step 2: Install Microsoft Authenticator
- Download the **Microsoft Authenticator** app on your mobile device.
- On Android, install the app from the personal side of the Google Play Store, not the work profile.
- If the app is already installed, skip this step.
